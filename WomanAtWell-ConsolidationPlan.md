## Replacement Story for Chapter 6: The Bleeding Woman

### Why This Story Fits Chapter 6 Better

**Chapter 6 Theme:** "Amazing Grace I Did Receive"
- Grace is UNEARNED, UNDESERVED, scandalous
- For wretches, not just people who stumbled
- Receiving vs. earning
- Opening your hands to accept what you can't deserve

**The Bleeding Woman Story (Mark 5:25-34, Luke 8:43-48):**

**Key Elements:**
1. **Desperate unworthiness:**
   - 12 years of bleeding = ceremonially unclean
   - Untouchable, isolated, broke (spent all on doctors)
   - No right to touch anyone, let alone a rabbi
   - Risk: touching Jesus could defile Him

2. **Bold, undeserving faith:**
   - "If I just touch his clothes, I will be healed"
   - She reaches out despite having NO right to
   - Doesn't ask permission (can't earn it)
   - Just reaches out in desperate need

3. **Scandalous grace received:**
   - Jesus doesn't rebuke her for making Him unclean
   - He stops, seeks her out
   - Calls her "Daughter" (family, belonging)
   - "Your faith has healed you" - she received what she couldn't earn
   - Immediate transformation: bleeding stops

4. **Perfect Chapter 6 alignment:**
   - She's a WRETCH (unclean, broke, desperate) - fits "saved a wretch like me"
   - She can't earn healing but reaches out anyway - fits "receiving vs. earning"
   - Grace given freely despite her unworthiness - fits "scandalous grace"
   - "Your faith" = trust in what she can't deserve - fits the chapter's call to receive

**Contrast with Woman at the Well:**
- Woman at the Well: Being SEEN/KNOWN and not condemned
- Bleeding Woman: Being UNDESERVING/UNCLEAN and healed anyway
- Woman at the Well fits Ch 4 (water's edge, being known)
- Bleeding Woman fits Ch 6 (amazing grace, unearned gift)

## New Chapter 6 Section: The Woman Who Touched Jesus

### Proposed Structure

**Section Title:** "The Woman Who Had No Right to Touch"

**Part 1: The Desperate Unworthy**
- 12 years of bleeding
- Spent everything on doctors, getting worse
- Ceremonially unclean = untouchable, isolated
- No right to be in a crowd, let alone touch a rabbi
- Risk: She could defile Jesus by touching Him

**Part 2: Faith That Reaches Anyway**
- "If I just touch his clothes, I will be healed"
- Not deserving, not clean, not worthy - but desperate
- She presses through the crowd
- Reaches out for the hem of His garment
- Receives what she has no right to ask for

**Part 3: Grace That Stops for the Undeserving**
- Jesus feels power go out
- He stops: "Who touched me?"
- She comes forward trembling (caught, exposed)
- Expecting rebuke for defiling Him
- Instead: "Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace."

**Part 4: What Grace Gives the Unworthy**
- Not just healing - BELONGING ("Daughter")
- Not just physical - spiritual restoration to community
- Not earned by worthiness - received by faith
- This is amazing grace: given to those who can't deserve it

**Connection to Chapter Theme:**
- "She had no right to touch Him. She was unclean, broke, desperate—a wretch by any religious standard. But grace doesn't wait for you to deserve it. Grace meets you in your desperation and says: 'Daughter, your faith has made you whole.'"

**Estimated Length:** ~40-50 lines
