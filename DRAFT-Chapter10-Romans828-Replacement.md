### 3. The Promise That Nothing Is Wasted

Remember Romans 8:28, which we explored earlier in this chapter: "And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

This is the bedrock promise: God is relentlessly committed to redeeming every moment of your story. Even the worst ones. Even the ones that feel utterly wasted.

Your pain has purpose. Your suffering isn't random. Your struggles aren't wasted.

God is working—actively, intentionally, lovingly—to bring good from it all.

> "God wastes nothing—not even sin. The soul that has struggled and come through is enriched by its struggle, and the grace of God is not frustrated."
> — Evelyn Underhill, *The Spiritual Life*
