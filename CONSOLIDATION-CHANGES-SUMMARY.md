**Why This Story Fits Chapter 6 Better:**

**Chapter 6 Theme:** "Amazing Grace I Did Receive"
- Grace is UNEARNED, UNDESERVED, scandalous
- For wretches, not just people who stumbled
- Receiving vs. earning

**Bleeding Woman Story Elements:**
1. Twelve years of bleeding = ceremonially unclean, untouchable
2. Broke, desperate, isolated (true wretch status)
3. No right to touch Jesus (could defile Him)
4. Desperate faith reaches anyway
5. Jesus calls her "Daughter" - belonging, not condemnation
6. "Your faith has healed you" (not worthiness)
7. Perfect illustration: grace for the undeserving

**Thematic Alignment:**
- Woman at the Well → Being SEEN/KNOWN (fits Ch 4)
- Bleeding Woman → Being UNDESERVING/GRACED (fits Ch 6)

**Result:** Better thematic fit, fresh content, no duplication
